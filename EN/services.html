Advanced Technology Solutions for Organizations

We provide a complete suite of IT, cloud, cybersecurity, development, and infrastructure services, fully tailored to your organization's needs, regulatory requirements, and business goals. We guide our clients from planning and design through implementation, maintenance, and future growth.

Managed IT Services

We provide end-to-end managed IT services, including user support, endpoint and server management, communication equipment, and enterprise computing systems. The service is based on continuous monitoring, proactive troubleshooting, and ongoing performance optimization, ensuring high availability, business continuity, and an optimal user experience.

IT Consulting, Planning & Strategy

We provide strategic consulting and planning services that combine long-term technological and business vision. The service includes infrastructure and systems mapping, cost review, analysis of third-party vendor agreements, identification of redundant services and licenses, and recommendations for efficiency and savings. Our goal is to build a secure, efficient, and cost-effective technology infrastructure tailored to your organization's needs and future growth.

Cloud, Backup & Disaster Recovery (DR)

We provide advanced cloud solutions that enable operational flexibility, work from anywhere, and efficient management of organizational resources. Our solutions include Microsoft 365, Azure, Google Workspace, private and hybrid cloud services, alongside backup and disaster recovery (DR) solutions that ensure business continuity, high resilience, and rapid recovery of systems and data in the event of a failure, cyberattack, or disaster.

Cybersecurity & Information Security

We provide comprehensive cybersecurity and information security solutions, including protection for endpoints, servers, and cloud environments through Firewall systems and advanced EDR/XDR solutions.

The service includes penetration testing (PT), risk assessments and privacy law compliance, permission management, procedure writing, and employee training, aimed at strengthening organizational defense and meeting regulatory requirements.

Virtualization, Servers & Data Centers

We build and manage advanced server environments based on VMware, Hyper-V, and other leading virtualization solutions. The service includes the setup and management of application servers, databases, file servers, and virtual environments, optimizing hardware resource utilization, improving performance, reducing costs, and ensuring high availability and resilience.

Custom Application & Systems Development

We develop custom applications, business systems, and digital solutions that help streamline workflows, automate processes, integrate existing systems, and support your organization's business growth.

Hardware, Software & Licensing Supply

As business partners of the world's leading manufacturers, we provide hardware, software, and licensing solutions precisely tailored to your organization's needs, including servers, storage systems, workstations, communication equipment, information security solutions, backup systems, and software licenses from the market's leading vendors.

Communication Infrastructure, Fiber Optics & Low Voltage

We specialize in planning and establishing advanced communication infrastructure, from deploying communication and fiber-optic infrastructure to setting up secure server rooms tailored to your organization's needs. In addition, we provide low-voltage solutions including access control systems, CCTV security cameras, wired and wireless networks, and end-to-end communication solutions, meeting the strictest standards.
