Products & Solutions

Strategic Consulting & Planning

Comprehensive site survey, consulting on choosing the most suitable infrastructure and connectivity for the organization's needs, project planning including detailed specifications for communication, hardware, and software systems.

Communication Infrastructure & Low Voltage

Planning and deployment of communication networks, endpoints, and server room establishment. Our expertise includes the installation of smart PBX (telephony) and low-voltage systems, including access control infrastructure and CCTV.

Virtualization & Servers

Setup and configuration of application and mail servers using VMware, Hyper-V, or Open Source technologies. These solutions enable optimal hardware resource utilization, maintenance cost savings, and maximum operational flexibility. Server configurations are tailored for organizations ranging from a few users to networks supporting hundreds of workstations.

Information Security

The majority of organizational data breaches are caused by users (usually in good faith). Proper training, establishing work procedures, and using the right technological tools reduce and even prevent damage from users. These are, of course, in addition to the correct definition and management of Firewall, EDR, and protection tools to prevent malicious network intrusion.

Cloud Computing

Provision of cloud-based mail services (Hosted Exchange), file servers, application servers, and terminal servers in physical or virtual cloud configurations. We offer Office 365 and Google Apps based solutions, customized to the organization's specific requirements.

Hardware & Software Supply

As authorized resellers of leading brands (Dell, Lenovo, Fortinet, Check Point, etc.), we provide servers, workstations, peripheral equipment, and software tailored precisely to your requirements.
