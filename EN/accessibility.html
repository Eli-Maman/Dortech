Accessibility Statement

A website for everyone

Our Commitment

Dortech Information Technology is committed to providing equal and accessible service to all customers and visitors, including people with disabilities. We have made this website accessible in accordance with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013, and Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on the WCAG 2.0 guidelines at level AA.

Accessibility Features

  • An accessibility menu (the ♿ button in the corner of the screen) offering larger text, high contrast, highlighted links, a readable font and stopping animations. Settings are kept as you move between pages.
  • Full keyboard navigation, with a clearly visible focus indicator and a "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
  • Screen reader support: a logical heading structure, alternative text for images and labels for every form field.
  • Fully responsive on phones and tablets, and support for the operating system's "reduce motion" setting.
  • Text and background color contrast that meets the standard.
  • The website is available in Hebrew and English.

Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some parts of the website may not be fully accessible. Client logos are displayed as images, and client names also appear as text. We continue to review and improve the accessibility of the website on an ongoing basis.
