Who We Are

Dortech Information Technology is a boutique company specializing in IT, cloud, cybersecurity, and infrastructure solutions for organizations in the business and institutional sectors. For over 25 years, we have supported companies, offices, and institutions with personal attention, high availability, and an in-depth understanding of each client's unique needs.

We operate under a One Stop Shop model, allowing our clients to receive all the technological services their organization needs under one roof. This saves the hassle of dealing with multiple vendors, with all technological activity managed by a single professional point of contact.

The extensive experience we've accumulated over the years allows us to tailor the exact solution for each client. We believe there is no one-size-fits-all solution, so we build a "technology suit" for every organization, tailored to its needs, business goals, and budget, while maximizing efficiency, performance, and long-term savings.

More Than a Service Provider. A True Technology Partner.

When we take responsibility for an organization's information systems, we become the central point of contact for every technological matter. After conducting a comprehensive systems survey and mapping the work environment, we centralize all activity with communication, software, hardware, and cloud vendors on your behalf.

We manage vendor relationships, review costs, identify redundant services, and continuously work to secure the most cost-effective and efficient solutions for your organization. Instead of dealing with multiple parties, you benefit from a single point of contact that takes full responsibility for all systems and services.

What this means for you is simple: less hassle, lower costs, and more time to focus on running your business.

Full Responsibility. Uncompromising Service.

Dortech's service model is based on taking total responsibility for the organization's computing environment. Under our retainer services, clients enjoy support, maintenance, and ongoing management of their entire IT environment for a fixed, known monthly cost.

We believe quality service is measured by results, not by hours worked. That's why we are committed to system availability, information security, business continuity, and customer satisfaction. Our proactive approach allows us to identify issues in advance, prevent failures, and maintain a stable, secure, and efficient work environment over time.

In addition, we provide outsourcing solutions for organizations that need IT managers, systems staff, or information systems managers, backed by the full support of Dortech's experts and the professional experience we've built over more than 25 years of activity.
