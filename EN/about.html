Who We Are

Dortech Information Technology Ltd. has been operating since 2001 with the goal of providing comprehensive computing solutions for businesses under the "One Stop Shop" concept. We provide a wide envelope of services including consulting, deployment, management, and maintenance of IT infrastructure, communication networks, hardware supply, and peripheral equipment.

Feel the Difference

When we take responsibility for a client's information systems, the difference is felt immediately. We are not just another external IT company providing technical services, from the very first moment, you will feel that Dortech is your in-house computer department.

Following a comprehensive site survey and a technological "alignment," we become your single point of contact (SPOC) for all third-party vendors: ISP, software providers, or any other technological supplier. This begins with securing the best deals and prices for you and continues with ongoing management and full resolution of technical issues. With us, you will no longer hear from the software provider that "the problem is with the hardware," or from the hardware provider that "the problem is with the software."

Every inquiry is made directly to Dortech, and we manage the event until a full solution is reached. We take the responsibility so you can focus on running your business with peace of mind.

Total Responsibility & Technological Security

We offer a retainer-based support program founded on taking total responsibility for the organization's computing layout. This model allows you to receive support and maintenance for a fixed monthly fee, with no limit on hours. This is the core of our reliability: we are committed to results, not to hours worked.

In addition to our retainer model, Dortech provides customized Outsourcing solutions for larger organizations. This includes placing professional IT experts, CIOs, or system administrators on a full-time or part-time basis within your organization. Every employee working at your site benefits from the guidance, professional backup, and extensive knowledge base of Dortech.
